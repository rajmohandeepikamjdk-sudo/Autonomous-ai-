How a cycle runs

app/agents/orchestrator.py
01

Topic discovery

Scores seed topics plus memory-derived ones; picks one not covered recently, checked against ChromaDB for similarity.

02

Source validation

Filters candidate sources against a domain allow/deny list and a reachability check before anything gets cited.

03

Web research

Gathers structured findings per validated source. Zero usable sources aborts the cycle rather than inventing content.

04

Content writing

Drafts a title, body, and rationale from the research notes — the "why this angle" is kept as a first-class field.

05

Quality review

Self-critiques the draft and can send it back for a bounded number of revisions before giving up on the cycle.

06

Fact-checking

Cross-checks every claim in the draft against the research notes and flags anything unsupported.

07

Publishing

Hashes the post to reject duplicates, persists it to SQLite, and embeds it into memory so future cycles don't repeat it.
